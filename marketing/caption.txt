3D LAB // 003 — Floor Plan → Walkthrough

A floor plan tells you where things go. Walking through the space helps you ask better questions.

In this experiment, you can explore a furnished apartment, compare two dining tables and see what changes: a bigger table leaves a smaller gap to the sofa.

The editable model takes you through every room along a continuous route. Higgsfield adds a 32-second room-by-room cinematic sequence with warm daylight, satin oak, matte plaster and soft linen.

Upload your own plan, use the optional AI extraction, review the dimensions and build a model you can explore. You can also export it to Blender.

This is a concept tool. Generated video can change details, so measurements come from the reviewed 3D model—not the film.

The source code is free, and the included sample works without keys. Optional AI extraction and new generations use paid services.
